The people behind SLGlobal

Sara Borodin   
Co-Founder & Managing Director

Sara Borodin is the Co-Founder and Managing Director of Strategic Leadership Global (SLGlobal), a woman-owned small business dedicated to helping organizations and governments drive transformative, sustainable results in complex environments.

Prior to founding SLGlobal, Sara served at the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID) as a member of the Senior Executive Service. Most recently, Sara served as the Deputy Assistant Administrator for Asia where she provided policy and strategic direction for a $5 billion portfolio in 35 countries in Asia and the Pacific. Sara also served in other senior leadership positions at USAID including as Director for the Office of Middle East Affairs. 

Sara is a Leadership Coach, having obtained her Executive Certificate in Leadership Coaching from Georgetown University in 2023\. As a recipient of coaching herself, Sara believes that everyone can benefit from coaching. She specializes in executive coaching, leadership development, and coaching careers in transition. 

Prior to joining USAID, Sara worked for a foreign policy think tank and the U.S. Institute of Peace in Washington D.C. She also worked as a program developer at a non-profit organization overseas. 

Sara holds a Master's degree in International Peace and Conflict Resolution from American University, and a Joint Honors Bachelor degree in Middle East Studies and Religious Studies from McGill University in Montreal, Canada. 

Sara can be contacted at [leadership@SLGlobal.org](mailto:leadership@SLGlobal.org)

Jessica Zaman

Co-Founder & Managing Director

Jessica Zaman is the Co-Founder and Managing Director of Strategic Leadership Global (SLGlobal), a woman-owned small business dedicated to helping organizations and governments drive transformative, sustainable results in complex environments.

Prior to founding SLGlobal, Jessica served at the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID) as a member of the Senior Foreign Service. Most recently, she was the Deputy Mission Director for USAID in the West Bank and Gaza, where she led a multi-sector portfolio and a 100-person team operating in a highly volatile environment. Previously, she served in other senior leadership positions including directing USAID’s Office of Middle East Affairs, where she helped restart U.S. assistance programs and operationalize the Middle East Partnership for Peace Act—mobilizing significant resources and aligning policy with implementation across missions.

Her work spans Africa, Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America, bringing deep cross-cultural expertise and global perspective. Prior to USAID, she worked on Capitol Hill, at an international elections NGO, and in government relations. She is a graduate of the University of Virginia.
